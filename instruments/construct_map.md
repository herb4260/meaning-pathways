# Construct map

All demo items are **synthetic placeholders**, not copyrighted clinical scales. They operationalize constructs for a reproducible methods prototype and must not be treated as validated instruments.

| Construct | Conceptual role | Example item domain |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning violation | Discrepancy between event meaning and global beliefs/goals | disrupted assumptions; blocked life goals |
| Meaning in life | Coherence, purpose, comprehensibility | life makes sense; future has direction |
| Positive R/S coping | Meaning-making effort using religious/spiritual resources | benevolent reappraisal; spiritual connection |
| R/S struggle | Conflict/tension in religious or spiritual meaning | abandonment; anger; doubt; guilt |
| Recovery/functioning | Adjustment outcome | hope, functioning, emotional steadiness, future orientation |
