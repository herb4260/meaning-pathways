# Ethics and Interpretation Boundaries

This repository uses only synthetic data. In real studies of trauma and spirituality:

- obtain institutional ethics review and informed consent;
- minimize collection of identifiable narratives;
- separate research assessment from clinical or pastoral judgment;
- do not classify spiritual experiences as healthy/pathological solely from religious content;
- include referral and safety procedures for distress, suicidality, coercion, or functional impairment;
- include community consultation when operationalizing religious/spiritual constructs;
- report null and adverse associations, not only beneficial ones.
