# Methods Notes

## Why RI-CLPM?
Traditional cross-lagged models can mix stable between-person differences with within-person temporal dynamics. The RI-CLPM adds random intercept factors to isolate stable person-level tendencies before estimating within-person autoregressive and cross-lagged paths.

## Why measurement invariance first?
Longitudinal change is hard to interpret when item loadings or intercepts change across waves. The project therefore treats invariance testing as a prerequisite to substantive latent change claims.

## Why growth and cross-lagged models together?
Growth models answer **how trajectories differ**. RI-CLPM answers **how deviations from one's own usual level predict later deviations**. They address different longitudinal questions.

## Why moderated mediation?
Positive R/S coping and spiritual struggle can coexist. A conditional indirect-effect model demonstrates how an ostensibly adaptive coping pathway may operate differently when spiritual conflict is elevated.
